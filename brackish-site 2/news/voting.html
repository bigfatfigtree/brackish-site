Early Voting Is Open. The Lines Are Not.

OCT 13, 2026BY THE BRACKISH DESK2 MIN READ

Here's your civic duty briefing, all killer no filler: early voting is open across all seven cities, and right now the polls are gloriously empty.

10 minThat's the in-and-out time at most early voting locations on a weekday morning. The people waiting until Election Day will stand in line for an hour. You are smarter than that.

What to know

  • Where: your city's registrar office, plus satellite locations — check your city's website for the full list.
  • When: weekdays during business hours, plus select Saturdays as Election Day approaches.
  • Bring: an acceptable ID. Virginia's list is broad — driver's license, utility bill, even a paycheck stub in some cases.

Why it matters here

The 757 is a military town, a port town, and seven cities that love to argue with each other — which means local races here actually move daily life: development, schools, the roads you sit on. The midterms are three weeks out. Ten minutes now beats an hour in November.

No endorsements, no lectures — just go vote. We'll have the results briefing the morning after.
