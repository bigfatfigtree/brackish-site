Movements on Halloween: The Only Correct Plan for Oct 31

OCT 13, 2026BY THE BRACKISH DESK2 MIN READ

Every year, Halloween presents the same dilemma: the house party that peaks at 11, the bar crawl that ends in regret, or the thing you'll actually remember. This year the answer is pre-printed: Movements play the NorVa on October 31.

The California post-hardcore band deals in exactly the kind of cathartic, throat-shredding singalongs that feel spiritually correct on Halloween. A thousand costumed people screaming their lungs out in a former vaudeville theater? That's not a concert, that's a ritual.

The rules

  • Costume mandatory in spirit, if not in dress code. Commit.
  • Get there early — Halloween shows at the NorVa do not stay un-sold-out.
  • Eat first. Downtown Norfolk on Halloween night is a contact sport.

Movements · Sat Oct 31 · The NorVa, 317 Monticello Ave, Norfolk. Tickets via the venue — don't wait on this one.
