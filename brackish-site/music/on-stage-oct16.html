Brackish On Stage: The Loudest Weekend of the Fall

OCT 13, 2026BY THE BRACKISH DESK4 MIN READ

This is the weekend the fall music calendar has been pointing at. Five real shows in four days, spanning pop-punk, metal, piano-rock royalty, New Orleans brass, and whatever The Residents are. Here's the rundown.

Wednesday: Trombone Shorty & Orleans Avenue

Sandler Center, Virginia Beach, 8pm. Troy Andrews brings the full New Orleans street-parade treatment to Town Center tomorrow night, with Abraham Alexander opening. If you've never seen a trombone lead a funk band like a prizefighter, fix that.

Saturday: The Story So Far (tour kickoff)

The Dome, Virginia Beach, 7:30pm. The pop-punk veterans kick off their entire North American tour right here, with The Starting Line and Ultra Q in support. Opening night of a tour in your town is a specific kind of energy — the band's fresh, the setlist's a mystery, and the room knows it. From about $53.

Saturday: Five Finger Death Punch + Cody Jinks

Veterans United Home Loans Amphitheater, Virginia Beach. The only October show at the amp, and it's a sledgehammer double bill: metal's most reliable road dogs plus country's most outlaw-adjacent headliner. Tailgate accordingly.

Saturday: Bruce Hornsby & The Noisemakers

Ferguson Center, Newport News. The Williamsburg native comes home. Hornsby's live shows are where the songs stretch out and get strange in the best way — a piano master with a jam-band heart and a songwriter's discipline.

Sunday: The Residents

Elevation 27, Virginia Beach. Fifty-plus years in, still anonymous, still eyeballs, still the weirdest night on any calendar they touch. With Rushadicus. Go in curious, come out converted or confused — both are correct.

The verdict

If you only do one: Saturday at The Dome is the tour-kickoff electricity. If you do two: add Trombone Shorty tomorrow and make it a whole thing. See you out there.

Quick answers

What concerts are in Hampton Roads this weekend?

The Story So Far (Sat, The Dome), Five Finger Death Punch (Sat, the amphitheater), Bruce Hornsby (Sat, Ferguson Center), Trombone Shorty (Wed, Sandler Center), and The Residents (Sun, Elevation 27).

What time does The Story So Far play at The Dome?

Saturday, October 17 at 7:30pm at The Dome, 400 20th St, Virginia Beach — it's the kickoff of their North American tour.

Where can I see live brass/funk this week?

Trombone Shorty & Orleans Avenue play the Sandler Center in Virginia Beach on Wednesday, October 14 at 8pm.
