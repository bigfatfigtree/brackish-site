AFI Brings 25 Years of Beautiful Darkness to the NorVa Tonight

OCT 13, 2026BY THE BRACKISH DESK3 MIN READ

Tonight at 7:30, the doors at 317 Monticello Ave open for AFI — the California punk band that spent the last three decades turning eyeliner and heartbreak into an arena-sized art form, and somehow never lost the plot.

If you grew up on "Miss Murder," you don't need the pitch. If you didn't: AFI is what happens when hardcore kids grow up, get ambitious, and decide the darkness deserves better production values. Their live show is famously theatrical — all shadows, strobes, and Davey Havok prowling the stage like the room owes him money.

Why this room matters

The NorVa is a former vaudeville theater turned 1,500-capacity rock room, and it's the best-sounding box in the 757 — big enough to feel like an event, small enough that there's genuinely not a bad spot. AFI in a room this size is the sweet spot: intimate enough for the singalongs, loud enough to rearrange your organs.

The verdict

Go. NorVa shows at this scale don't sit around — and Thursday the same stage flips completely for All Them Witches' heavy Nashville psych-rock. Two nights, two universes, one room. That's the whole argument for living in a city with a venue like this.

AFI · Tue Oct 13, 7:30pm · The NorVa, 317 Monticello Ave, Norfolk. Tickets via the venue. Doors early, curfew never.
