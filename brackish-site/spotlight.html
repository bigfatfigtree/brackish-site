This Week: Maya R., Pitmaster & Owner, Smoked Out Norfolk

Interview · October 2026 · Sample edition — real interviews begin at launch.

1. What are you building in the 757?
A barbecue joint that treats brisket like it matters — because it does. Twelve-hour smokes, no shortcuts, no gas assist. Norfolk deserves better than chain 'cue.

2. What's the biggest misconception about your industry here?
That Hampton Roads doesn't have a food scene. We do — it's just spread across seven cities and nobody's connecting the dots. That's literally your job, so get to it.

3. Where do you eat when nobody's watching?
A certain taco truck off Tidewater Drive that I'm not naming, because the line is already too long.

4. What's one thing Hampton Roads needs?
Later hours. This town runs on shift work and the kitchens close at nine. Somebody fix that and I'll cater your grand opening for free.

5. Where can people find you?
Smoked Out, Granby Street, Thursday through Sunday. When the brisket's gone, it's gone — and it goes.
