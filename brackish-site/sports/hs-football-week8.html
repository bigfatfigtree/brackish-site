Friday Night Lights: Six of the State's Top Ten Are From the 757

OCT 13, 2026BY THE BRACKISH DESK4 MIN READ

Let's start with the number that matters: six of the ten best teams in Virginia's Class 5 play in the 757. Not the state — our corner of it. Maury sits at No. 1, King's Fork at No. 2, Indian River at No. 3, Bethel at No. 6, Green Run at No. 8, and Cox at No. 10, with Salem and Kellam getting honorable-mention love. If you live anywhere else in Virginia, this is the part where you sigh.

Last week: not close

Friday night was a parade of running clocks. Maury hung 64 on Norview — 64 to nothing — moving to 4-2 and looking every bit the state's top team. Salem dropped 69 on First Colonial. Indian River handled Nansemond River 41-13 in a Southeastern District statement. Oscar Smith bounced back from two straight losses to throttle Deep Creek 54-7. Green Run beat Landstown 40-13, and Bayside took down Tallwood 33-12 in Beach District play.

The Maury number is the one to sit with. The Commodores opened the season 2-2 against a brutal out-of-state schedule — and have since outscored opponents by a combined 122-6 over two weeks. The poll had them No. 1 at 2-2. At 4-2, they're not giving it back.

This Friday: where to be

  • Maury @ I.C. Norcom. The No. 1 team goes on the road in the Eastern District. Norcom's building something; Maury's building a highlight reel.
  • Oscar Smith @ Grassfield. The Tigers (3-2-1) are rolling again after the Deep Creek rout. Southeastern District positioning is on the line.
  • King's Fork @ Western Branch. The No. 2 team in Class 5 against a Branch squad that's taken lumps from Phoebus, Indian River, and Nansemond River. Trap game or coronation — that's why they play.
  • Nansemond River vs. Hickory. The Warriors need a bounce-back after Indian River. Hickory's been frisky.

The state of the districts

Eastern: Maury's league to lose, and everyone knows it. Southeastern: the deepest top-to-bottom district in the region — Indian River, King's Fork, Oscar Smith, and Nansemond River all capable of beating each other on the right night. Beach: Green Run and Cox carrying the banner, Salem and Kellam lurking. Peninsula: Bethel's 5-0 start has the Bruins dreaming big, and Phoebus remains Phoebus until proven otherwise.

Why we're doing this

College ball gets the TV money and the marching bands get the halftime show, but Friday night is the 757's real civic religion — the one where the whole town shows up. So we're covering it like it matters: scores, standings, and the games worth your Friday, every week through the playoffs. See you under the lights.
