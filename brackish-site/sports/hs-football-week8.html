Friday Night Lights: Six of the State's Top Ten Are From the 757

OCT 13, 2026BY THE BRACKISH DESK4 MIN READ

Let's start with the number that matters: six of the ten best teams in Virginia's Class 5 play in the 757. Maury sits at No. 1, King's Fork at No. 2, Indian River at No. 3, Bethel at No. 6, Green Run at No. 8, and Cox at No. 10. And in the statewide top 25, the 757 holds six of the top fifteen. If you live anywhere else in Virginia, this is the part where you sigh.

Oh, and Maury is chasing a fourth consecutive state title — which would make the Commodores the first South Hampton Roads team ever to do it. The poll had them No. 1 at 2-2 after a brutal out-of-state schedule. At 4-2, they're not giving it back.

Last week: history in Hampton

The result of the weekend didn't come Friday — it came Saturday, when Bethel beat Phoebus for the first time in 30 years. Three decades. Bethel led 21-0 at halftime and ended a drought that predates most of the players' parents' high school graduations. Freshman quarterback Tyson Carter led a young core that just announced itself.

Friday's slate was a parade of running clocks: Maury 64, Norview 0. Indian River 41, Nansemond River 13 — the Braves are 6-0. King's Fork 40, Western Branch 7 — the Bulldogs are 5-0. Oscar Smith 54, Deep Creek 7, a bounce-back after two straight losses. Green Run 21, Kellam 14. Salem 35, First Colonial 0. Bayside 33, Tallwood 12. And Booker T. Washington 55, Granby 0 — the Bookers are 4-2 and lurking in the Eastern District.

This week: where to be

  • Thu: Phoebus at Hampton. A Peninsula rivalry with Phoebus trying to stop a skid after the Bethel loss. Thursday night lights — rare and worth it.
  • #3 Indian River (6-0) at Deep Creek. The Braves have been inevitable. Deep Creek gets the next crack at stopping them.
  • #4 Maury (4-2) at Norcom (3-3), 7pm Friday. The No. 1 team in Class 5 goes on the road in the Eastern District.
  • #8 King's Fork (5-0) at Lakeland. The Bulldogs keep rolling through the Southeastern slate.
  • #9 Green Run (6-1) at First Colonial. Beach District business.
  • #10 Oscar Smith (3-2-1) at Grassfield. Southeastern positioning on the line — the Tigers are rolling again.
  • #15 Bethel vs. Woodside. The Bruins' first game as the team that finally beat Phoebus. The whole Peninsula is watching.
  • Booker T. Washington (4-2) at Norview (4-3). Eastern District with real stakes.

Players to watch

  • Marcus Chapman, QB, Hampton (sr.). 8-of-10 for 240 yards and 4 TDs passing — plus a pick-six on defense — in a 68-8 win over Menchville. Yes, both ways.
  • Dominick Everette, QB, Maury (jr.). Five touchdown passes in a 56-28 win over Bishop McNamara. The engine of the four-peat chase.
  • Jeremiah Jones, WR, Denbigh (sr.). Four catches, 136 yards, 2 TDs in Denbigh's 33-24 upset of Hampton.
  • Valen-Tino Shaw, Indian River (jr.). Nine catches for 100 yards and a TD against Oscar Smith — plus a sack and a fumble recovery on defense.
  • Tyson Carter, QB, Bethel (fr.). A freshman who just ended a 30-year drought. Remember the name.

The state of the districts

Eastern: Maury's league to lose, with Booker T. Washington and Norview chasing. Southeastern: the deepest top-to-bottom district in the region — Indian River, King's Fork, and Oscar Smith all capable of beating each other on the right night. Beach: Green Run and Cox carrying the banner. Peninsula: Bethel's the story now, Phoebus is wounded, and Hampton's got the most exciting quarterback in the district.

Which 757 teams are ranked in Virginia Class 5?

Yes — Bethel beat Phoebus on Saturday, October 10, ending a three-decade drought. Bethel led 21-0 at halftime behind freshman QB Tyson Carter.
