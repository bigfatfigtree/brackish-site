About Brackish

A closer look at the place we call home.

HAMPTON ROADS, VIRGINIA

Brackish is where the region's currents meet — fresh and salt, old and new, Norfolk and Virginia Beach and everywhere in between. We're a local newsletter and magazine-style site covering the people, places, food, music and culture shaping Hampton Roads and the surrounding coast.

We're independent, reader-first, and allergic to boring. Every issue is built to be read in minutes and forwarded in seconds.

Editorial Standards

  • AI-assisted, human-edited. AI does the research and drafting; a human who lives here makes every call on what runs.
  • Sources cited. Numbers and claims get attribution. If we can't source it, we don't print it.
  • Opinions labeled. "The Take" is opinion — argued with evidence, never disguised as news.
  • Edgy, not cruel. We punch up at institutions and bad ideas. We don't punch down at people.
  • Corrections run fast. Get something wrong? We fix it and say so, in the next issue.

The Plan

Hampton Roads is edition one. The brand is built to travel — Brackish: Richmond, Brackish: Charleston, Brackish: wherever the water meets the land and the stories are good.
