Virginia's First Dutch Bros Opens Tomorrow on Lynnhaven. Expect a Line.

OCT 15, 2026BY THE BRACKISH DESK3 MIN READ

Virginia held out longer than almost anyone. Dutch Bros — the Oregon-born drive-thru coffee chain with roughly 1,300 locations and a fan base that treats new openings like album drops — had stores in 25 states before it bothered with this one. That ends tomorrow, Friday, October 16, when the state's first Dutch Bros opens at 796 Lynnhaven Parkway in Virginia Beach.

If you've never encountered one: there is no café. No tables, no Wi-Fi, no guy typing a screenplay. It's drive-thru only — coffee, freezes, lemonades, teas, smoothies, and the Rebel energy drinks that built the cult — served by famously upbeat “broistas” out of a window roughly the size of a toll booth. Doors (windows?) open at 5 a.m., closing at 10 p.m. Sunday through Thursday and 11 p.m. on Fridays and Saturdays.

The Peninsula is next

Lynnhaven is the beachhead, not the whole invasion. Dutch Bros has plans in York County, where a drive-thru is slated for 5601–5619 George Washington Memorial Highway in Grafton — the current site of The Chipper — and the company applied for a special use permit in Williamsburg back in March. Rival 7 Brew is circling Route 17 too. The drive-thru coffee wars have officially reached Hampton Roads; Lynnhaven Parkway is just the first battlefield.

What it means for local coffee

Here's the honest read: Dutch Bros doesn't compete with your favorite local roaster on craft. It competes on speed, sweetness, and a line of idling cars at 6 a.m. What it sells is a habit, and habits are hard to break. The local shops' moat was never caffeine — it's the room, the regulars, the laptop-and-linger ritual Dutch Bros doesn't even attempt. If anything, a chain this loud planting its first Virginia flag in Virginia Beach says something real about where the growth is.

Practical advice for tomorrow: the opening-day line at a first-in-state Dutch Bros is a known phenomenon. If you're going, go early or go late. If you're just trying to get down Lynnhaven Parkway — Godspeed.

Quick answers

When does Dutch Bros open in Virginia Beach?

Friday, October 16, 2026, at 796 Lynnhaven Parkway. It is the first Dutch Bros location in Virginia.

What are the hours?

5 a.m. to 10 p.m. Sunday through Thursday, and 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. Friday and Saturday. The location is drive-thru only.

Is Dutch Bros coming to Yorktown?

Yes — a location is planned for 5601–5619 George Washington Memorial Highway in Grafton (York County), and the company has also pursued a site in Williamsburg.
