"Champagne & Sin": Norfolk's Burlesque Night Is Not Kidding Around

OCT 13, 2026BY THE BRACKISH DESK2 MIN READ

Your Saturday night options are usually: the bar, the other bar, or the couch. This Saturday, there's a fourth: "Champagne & Sin" — burlesque plus live music at 37th and Zen, 8pm — and it's the most glamorous room in Norfolk that night, no contest.

Burlesque done right is athletic, funny, and genuinely theatrical — closer to vaudeville than anything you'd expect. Paired with a live band instead of a playlist, this one has live-wire energy that a DJ set can't touch.

Dress like you mean it. The room will.

Know before you go

  • When: Saturday, 8pm. Doors earlier — get there for a good seat.
  • Where: 37th and Zen, Norfolk.
  • The move: dinner first in the neighborhood, then the show. Make a night of it instead of a drive-by.
