She Taught Second Grade. Now She's Betting Virginia Beach Still Reads.

OCT 15, 2026BY THE BRACKISH DESK3 MIN READ

Elisabeth Biswas spent five years as an educator, most recently teaching second grade. This summer she signed a lease instead. In late September, her bookstore — Orchard & Ink — opened in the Woodtide Shopping Center on Princess Anne Road, all 1,000 square feet of it, in the year of our algorithm 2026. A print bookstore. Opened on purpose.

The idea started in 2022, on a long phone call with her sister, venting about work and daydreaming about being her own boss. The pitch she landed on: combine the two things she loves — books and cider — and build a shop out of it. It sat in the “one day” file for three years. In March, she decided to actually do it. By July, she had a lease.

What's inside

The shop carries a mix of new and gently used books, plus notebooks, cards, and bags, styled with what she calls a 1990s coastal country theme — think the bookstore from a movie you'd rent on VHS. True to the name, there's nonalcoholic cider on offer. And the shelves aren't only hers: Orchard & Ink stocks custom book-themed candles from Scholar's Wick Candle Co. and handmade jewelry from Salty Sun Club, two other local small businesses getting shelf space instead of a landlord's cut of nothing.

The calendar is the point. Biswas is planning themed nights, book release events, local author nights, and craft nights — the stuff that turns a store into a room people actually gather in.

Why this matters more than a ribbon cutting

Every few months someone writes the obituary for the independent bookstore, and every few months somebody like Biswas opens one anyway. The math that works isn't competing with a warehouse in Kentucky on price — it's being a third place. A room with armchairs, cider, candles that smell like old libraries, and a reason to show up on a Tuesday. Strip centers across the 757 are full of vacant bays; the ones that come back to life look like this — small, specific, and run by someone who clearly loves the thing.

Orchard & Ink is at 4676 Princess Anne Rd in Virginia Beach. Go buy a book from a person. It still works.
